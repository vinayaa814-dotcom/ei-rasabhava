Participant information and consent

What this study is about

This study is developing a way to understand how people recognise and understand feelings in themselves and others. You will read short everyday scenarios and use an emotion wheel to mark feelings that capture what is happening in that situation.

What you will do

After one practice item, you will see short scenarios. For each one, tap the emotions on the wheel that fit, then rate how strong each one is (Mild, Moderate, Strong or Very Strong).

You are free to pick and rate as many emotions as you wish. There is no limit, and there are no right or wrong answers.

Taking part is voluntary

You do not have to take part. You may stop at any point by closing this page, without any consequence, and nothing is saved or sent unless you press Submit at the end.

You are asked to answer every scenario so that your set of responses is complete. However, you are never required to answer a scenario that upsets you. A Distress button is shown at the top of every screen. Tap it to flag the scenario and move on without answering, or to stop the survey. You will not be asked to explain.

Possible discomfort

Some scenarios describe upsetting situations, such as loss, accidents, conflict, or harm to animals. If a scenario bothers you, use the Distress button to flag it and move on, or stop. Support information is shown when you tap the button.

Your information

  • We do not ask for your name or phone number. Your initials are optional. If you enter them, they are stored with your answers.
  • At the very end you may, if you wish, leave an email address so that you can be contacted about this study. It is saved separately from your answers and is not attached to them. You can leave it blank.
  • We record the emotions you select, and your intensity ratings.
  • Your answers stay on your device until you press Submit at the end. Data collected are used only for research purposes. Results will be reported anonymously.

How the survey works

There are short scenarios. You can stop at any time. If a scenario upsets you, tap the Distress button at the top to flag it and move on.

Pick and rate as many emotions as you wish. There is no limit and no right or wrong answer.
  1. Read each scenario carefully, and look at the emotion wheel shown with it.
  2. Tap every emotion on the wheel that fits what happens in the situation. You can pick one, a few, or many. Tap an emotion again to un-pick it.
  3. Each emotion you pick appears in a list below the wheel. Rate each one as Mild, Moderate, Strong or Very Strong. Every emotion you pick needs a rating before you move on.
  4. Swipe the scenario left or right, or use Back and Next, to move between scenarios.
  5. Please answer every scenario before you submit. You can jump between scenarios in any order. If a scenario upsets you, tap ⚠ Distress at the top to flag it and move on, or to stop.
